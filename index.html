Interforge KI-C Premium Solution

Released agents that are graphite-free, being water-based, the environmental impact is minimal, keeping the operators and the press electrical equipment safe. Because of its composition of sodium-based acids and salts that are water-soluble, it generates no toxic fumes and no slippery surfaces that could endanger operator's physical integrity.

The dosage method is recommended via automatic spraying systems (like the RO3 system). This ensures that the dosage can be precisely controlled and held constant at every application cycle, reducing overall release agent consumption.

✓ Key Advantages

  • Water-based & graphite-free
  • Non-flammable (0% fire risk)
  • No toxic fumes or slippery dust
  • 100% automated dosing (RO3)

✗ Constraints

  • Requires automatic spray setup
  • Higher upfront implementation
Safety & Environment Excellent (5/5)
Productivity & Quality Maximum (5/5)
Consumption Efficiency Low Consumption (5/5)
Ease of Use Exceptional (5/5)

Acetylene Flame Soot coating

The process involves initiating the combustion of acetylene in front of a billet, resulting in the creation of carbon black. This carbon black serves as a release agent during the extrusion process. The procedure is automated, with the flame igniting as the billet transitions from the preheating furnace to the extrusion press.

✓ Key Advantages

  • Fully automated process
  • Cheap raw material cost

✗ Constraints

  • 100nm soot inhalation risk
  • Dark marks prevent anodizing
  • Open flame fire hazards
  • Requires separate coating per billet
Safety & Environment Critical (1/5)
Productivity & Quality Medium (3/5)
Consumption Efficiency Low Consumption (5/5)
Ease of Use Difficult (2/5)

Boron Nitride Suspension Liquid BN

Boron Nitride water-based suspension. BN exhibits strong adhesion at high temperatures. Coating a billet with BN ensures that the layer in contact with the dummy block adheres, reducing the frequency of re-coating to every third or fifth billet.

✓ Key Advantages

  • Strong adhesion at high temp
  • Water-soluble, non-flammable
  • Coating every 3rd to 5th billet

✗ Constraints

  • Stabilizers can generate blisters
  • High viscosity (larger vol/shot)
  • Requires manual supervision
Safety & Environment Good (3.7/5)
Productivity & Quality Good (3.7/5)
Consumption Efficiency Medium (4/5)
Ease of Use Moderate (3/5)

Boron Nitride Powder Dry Powder

Dry Boron Nitride powder spraying. Requires electrostatic charge on finely ground particles for adhesion. Managing airborne dispersion is extremely difficult as fine particles float over long distances.

✓ Key Advantages

  • Strong adhesion at high temp
  • Clean, dry workspace
  • Zero flammability / fire hazard

✗ Constraints

  • Highest material cost ($/kg)
  • Operator dust inhalation risk
  • Severe clogging on spray nozzles
  • Requires exclusively dry air or N2
Safety & Environment High Risk (4.2/5)
Productivity & Quality Good (3.7/5)
Consumption Efficiency Medium (3/5)
Ease of Use Difficult (1.7/5)

Graphite Suspension Conductive Paste

Lubricants formulated with graphite and organic binders. The flammable organic carrier ignites upon contact with hot tooling, generating massive smoke and soot that can lead to blister holes.

✓ Key Advantages

  • High thermal tool protection
  • Excellent release performance

✗ Constraints

  • Messy workspace (grease stains)
  • Ignition smoke generates blisters
  • Conductive (risks electrical short)
  • Fouls hot shears exit (unusable there)
Safety & Environment High Risk (3.5/5)
Productivity & Quality Maximum (5/5)
Consumption Efficiency Medium (4/5)
Ease of Use Difficult (3/5)

Sticks & Blocks Solid Lubricant

Solid graphite or Boron Nitride sticks applied manually to the dummy block or billet. While cheap to buy, it has the poorest overall control and highest safety risk in extrusion plants.

✓ Key Advantages

  • Extremely low product cost
  • Zero system investment cost

✗ Constraints

  • Highly dangerous manual application
  • Extreme risk of hot component burns
  • Excess application causes scrap
  • Messy work area, high VOCs
Safety & Environment Dangerous (1.7/5)
Productivity & Quality Low (1.7/5)
Consumption Efficiency Very High Consumption (1/5)
Ease of Use Manual / Messy (2.7/5)
