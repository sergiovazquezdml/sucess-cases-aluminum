## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Interforge KI-C is a water-based, graphite-free aluminum extrusion release agent delivered by the RO3 automated dosing system — zero residue, zero operator contact, measurable results.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Manual and uncontrolled lubrication methods in aluminum extrusion create simultaneous safety, quality, cost, and productivity failures — and operators have no way to improve one without worsening another.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- **Boron nitride (powder):** Applied by hand near 600 °C metal — inhalation risk, residue on profiles, no dosing control
- **Acetylene flames:** Open fire on the production floor, forced stops every 4 cycles, production loss accumulates to tens of thousands of lb/year
- **Graphite agents:** Black contamination, environmental issues, profile surface quality affected
- **Ejecteze and similar:** No dosing system means no measurement — consumption is double what's needed
**What it costs them:** Safety incidents (medical, legal, downtime), profile defects and scrap, untracked lubricant waste, production time lost to lubrication stops.
**Emotional tension:** "We know it's a problem but changing means stopping production, justifying capital, and trusting that the new system actually works better."

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Ejecteze, Bonderite, other water-based release agents — fall short because they're still applied manually or without a controlled dosing system, so consumption is high and results are inconsistent.
**Secondary:** Boron nitride powder — fall short because of operator safety risk, profile contamination, and zero dose control.
**Indirect:** Acetylene flame arms — fall short because of fire risk, forced downtime every 4 cycles, and regulatory exposure.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Water-based and graphite-free — no residue, no fumes, non-flammable
- Delivered by RO3 automated dosing — same exact volume every cycle, no operator contact
- In-motion application — no press stops for lubrication
- Independently signalled lubrication points — each point activates 1 or 2 valves per independent signal, enabling precise control across multiple press zones
- Validated by Interlub technical team on-site — results are measured and documented
**How we do it differently:** We treat lubrication as an engineering problem, not a consumable purchase. The product and the dosing system are co-designed: Interforge KI-C is formulated for in-motion spray application, and the RO3 system is calibrated to deliver the minimum effective dose at each specific lubrication point.
**Why customers choose us:** The combination of eliminating health hazards, recovering production time, and reducing consumption — all documented — gives purchasing and plant management a business case that is hard to dispute.
